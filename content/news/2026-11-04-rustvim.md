---
title: "Toward release 0.1.0"
date: 2026-11-04
category: news
---

Rustvim is working toward release **0.1.0**.

The project is incrementally moving editor internals to Rust while preserving
established editing behavior and plugin interfaces. Compatibility and focused
testing guide the migration.

The website is taking shape too, with a Rustvim logo, a rust-colored theme,
and a new home at [rustvim.org](https://rustvim.org/).

Follow development in the [Rustvim repository](https://github.com/tupos/rustvim)
and see the [roadmap](/roadmap/) for the current direction.

Earlier Neovim announcements are preserved in the
[upstream archive](/news/archive/) as historical context.
